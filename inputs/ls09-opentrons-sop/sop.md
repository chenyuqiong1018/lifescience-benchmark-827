# 24-sample magnetic-bead cleanup (frozen local-extension fixture)

The processing plate already contains 50 uL of sample in wells A1–D6 (24 samples).
For each sample, in column-major order:

1. Add 80 uL lysis buffer and mix 5 times with 100 uL.
2. Incubate 5 minutes.
3. Resuspend the bead reservoir, add 120 uL bead suspension, and mix 10 times with 180 uL.
4. Incubate 5 minutes, engage the Magnetic Module, and wait 7 minutes.
5. Remove 250 uL supernatant to liquid waste without disturbing beads.
6. Keeping the magnet engaged, wash twice: add 180 uL wash buffer, wait 30 seconds, and remove 180 uL to liquid waste.
7. Air-dry 2 minutes, disengage the magnet, add 40 uL elution buffer, and mix 10 times with 30 uL.

Tip policy is part of the frozen procedure. For each sample, use six independent tips: one fresh tip for lysis addition/mixing; one fresh tip for bead addition/mixing; one fresh tip for supernatant removal; one fresh tip for wash 1 addition and subsequent wash 1 removal; one fresh tip for wash 2 addition and subsequent wash 2 removal; and one fresh tip for elution addition/mixing. A wash tip may remain attached during its 30-second wait and be reused only to remove liquid from that same sample in that same wash cycle. Never return any tip that has contacted a sample well to a reagent reservoir. Thus the run requires 144 tips. Abort before execution if the deck, pipette, reagent volume, well capacity, tip count, or module compatibility is invalid.
